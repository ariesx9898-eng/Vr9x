### Conventions (Spec sections 6-7)

- Metres, Blender Z-up (the glTF exporter writes Y-up), one mesh node per file named after the asset, no node transforms. Slots use the Spec palette names verbatim; each GLB material carries a sensible base colour only (no textures). Card materials are exported double-sided, solids single-sided.
- Pivot: base centre on the ground (z = 0). Trunks, roots and rocks continue below ground (`min_z` in the manifest: 0.5-1.9 m for trees, 3-20 % of the total height for rocks) so nothing floats on slopes; ground-cover clumps start 0.02-0.06 m below z = 0.
- UVs: rocks and other solids are box-projected at 1 UV = 1 m. Bark is cylindrical: V = metres along the branch; U wraps an integer number of 1 m tiles on limbs at least 0.75 m round (seamless), and exactly one metre per metre of circumference on thinner limbs (cone unwrap, seam at the back). Cards (leaves, needles, palm fronds, grass, wheat, flowers, fern, reeds) cover the whole 0..1 texture with V pointing up or out along the frond.
- Normals: tree / bush leaf cards carry custom normals blended toward their crown (or canopy-mass) ellipsoid, giving soft, painterly volume shading; each card's winding is flipped when needed so its front face agrees with those normals. Grass-type clumps keep geometric normals. Rocks are smooth with sharp edges above ~40 deg (obsidian crags ~25 deg, flat-faceted).
- Snow is real geometry in `MT_Snow`: pillow sheets along every snow-pine bough, lumps on `SM_Bush_Snow_A`, a mound under `SM_Grass_Snow_A`, and cap sheets on `SM_Rock_Snow_*` whose edges tuck under the `MT_RockSnow` surface (no coincident faces). Mossy rocks switch up-facing faces to `MT_RockMossy`. Palm trunks use `MT_BarkDead` (grey, ringed geometry) and their hanging dead fronds `MT_GrassDry`, because the palette has no palm bark.
- Budgets: trees 2-10k tris (giants up to 20k), rocks 300-3k, grass / flower / fern / reed / wheat clumps < 400; bushes and dry scrub 150-2k, giant mushrooms and demon plants 150-3k.
- Manifest extras for placement tools: `regions` (Spec section 3 ids), `scatter` (which world density map should drive it: `Trees`, `Bushes`, `Grass`, `Flowers`, `Rocks`; `Cliffs` and `Landmark` for hand / rule placement), `size_range` + `size_rule`, `tris_by_material`, `bounds`, `notes`.

### QA

`qa_nature_kit.py` reads every GLB with its own numpy glTF parser (no Blender) and checks: triangle budget, size range, pivot on the ground at the base centre, no node transforms, slot names in the palette, no zero-area or degenerate triangles, no loose vertices, no duplicate / coincident triangles, unit normals that agree with the triangle winding, positive signed volume for solids (outward normals), solid texel density ~1 UV/m, and card UVs inside and spanning 0..1. Latest run: **74 / 74 assets pass (0 with warnings only)**.

Budget totals: Trees 103021 tris, Plants 16461 tris, Rocks 39303 tris.

### Limits and hand-off notes

- The contact sheets use procedural stand-in textures (leaf clusters, needle sprays, grass blades, flowers) and procedural bark / rock shading so the cards read as foliage; the game's real textures (`SourceArt/Textures/T_<Name>_*`) and material instances are mapped by slot name at import.
- Not verified in Unreal from this tool (the engine is not run here): import settings to use are "import normals" (keep the custom foliage normals), two-sided masked materials for the card slots, and "combine meshes" / one static mesh per GLB. No LODs or wind vertex colours are authored; Nanite or auto-LOD suit the rocks, and foliage wind can key off height or UV V.
- Leaf cards are intersecting quads by design (standard game foliage). Clumps sit at branch tips and on the crown shell, away from trunks; the exception is the conifers' pair of crossed core cards every ~2 m of trunk, which hug the trunk inside the bough mass so spruces never look hollow from afar.

### Conventions (Spec sections 6-7)

- Metres, Blender Z-up (the glTF exporter writes Y-up), +X right, **fronts / doors face -Y**. One mesh node per file named after the asset, transforms applied (QA checks identity node transforms).
- Pivot (`pivot` in the manifest): `base` = centre of the XY bounding box at ground level z = 0; `base_point` = a documented attachment point at z = 0 (wall axis, tower axis, bridge centre, flame root, ...); `center` = bounding-box centre (VFX); `hub` = windmill blades rotation centre.
- Buildings sit on a buried foundation skirt (plinth / body continue down to `min_z`, typically -0.5 m; walls -1.0 m; landmarks up to -1.5 m; bridges reach the river bed) so they never float on gently sloping ground. Ground floors are raised 0.25-0.9 m with steps to the door.
- UVs: box-projected at **1 UV unit = 1 m** on all architecture; roof slopes use a planar projection along the slope (u horizontal, v up-slope, still 1 UV = 1 m); cylindrical surfaces unwrap by arc length. VFX meshes use the explicit layouts in the VFX table.
- Material slots use the Spec palette names verbatim (MT_Plaster, MT_Timber, MT_RoofRed, ...); the GLB materials only carry preview base colours.
- No coincident faces, by construction: every part is a closed (or buried-back) shell that is sunk a few centimetres into whatever it touches, and overlapping attachments use distinct proud depths (e.g. corner posts 0.07 m, posts 0.06, rails 0.05, braces 0.04/0.035, window frames 0.03, sills 0.08). Openings are real recessed pockets in the wall solids (glass / door leaf at the back, reveals on the sides), not decals. Roof tiles are stepped courses in the roof section; snow blankets are separate solids whose underside is buried under the tile valleys and whose top clears the lips and ridge caps.
- Budgets: houses 1-6k tris; large buildings (manor, keep, guild, inn courtyard, caravan yard, chapel, hall) up to 15k; wall pieces up to 8k (gates 12k); props up to 4k; bridges up to 6k; landmarks up to 40k; VFX up to 3k.
